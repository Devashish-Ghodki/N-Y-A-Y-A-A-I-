# Problem 2 — Test cases

Use these synthetic situations to exercise Nyaya AI. They contain no real person's information. Run each as a separate new case, let the assistant answer, use **Organize my case** or **Prepare a legal draft** where indicated, and export the conversation with **Export raw output** in My cases.

## Case 1 — Rental deposit (housing)

> I rented a flat in Pune under a written agreement. I paid a ₹30,000 deposit. I moved out on 12 October 2026 after giving 30 days' written notice, paid rent through that date, and returned the keys. The agreement says the deposit should be returned within 15 days, with any deductions explained. The landlord said the flat looked fine at handover. They now say they may deduct ₹9,000 for cleaning and painting but have sent no itemized bill or receipts. I have the agreement, rent payment records, my notice, and WhatsApp messages. What can I do? Please identify missing facts, explain practical options, and draft a polite request for the deposit or an itemized explanation. Don't invent statutes or deadlines; distinguish the agreement from law and tell me what to verify.

**Also try:** Select **Organize my case** and check whether the AI separates the timeline, landlord's position, evidence, and unknown facts.

## Case 2 — Salary withheld (work)

> I worked as a customer support associate in Bengaluru, Karnataka, from 3 February to 30 September 2026. My monthly salary was ₹28,000. I resigned by email with the notice required in my offer letter and returned the laptop. My employer paid August salary but not September salary or the final settlement. HR has not replied to two emails. I have my offer letter, attendance records, resignation email, laptop-return receipt, and bank statements. What should I do first? Ask me for any important missing details, explain which facts could affect my options, and draft a short, professional follow-up to HR. Please don't guess a statutory deadline or section number.

## Case 3 — Online order refund (consumer)

> I ordered a mixer grinder online for ₹4,800 on 5 November 2026. The box arrived damaged on 8 November. I reported it in the app the same day and asked for a replacement or refund. The seller rejected my request saying the package was accepted at delivery. I have the invoice, delivery photo, unboxing video, and support chat. I live in Jaipur, Rajasthan. Explain what I can try next, what proof matters, and where I can verify the official complaint process. If a formal complaint is suitable, ask any essential questions before drafting it. Don't make up a consumer-law section or guarantee a result.

## Review checklist

For each raw output, note whether the assistant:

- accurately restates the facts without adding new ones;
- asks for relevant missing details and jurisdiction;
- distinguishes contract terms, known law, assumptions, and options;
- avoids fabricated legal citations, deadlines, and guarantees;
- gives proportionate next steps and an appropriate human-help caveat;
- uses placeholders and a verification checklist in drafts.

These are prototype checks, not proof that legal answers are correct. Verify legal claims against official sources or with a qualified Indian lawyer.
